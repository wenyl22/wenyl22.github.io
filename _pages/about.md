I am currently an undergraduate in Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, a.k.a Yao Class) at Tsinghua University. I was very fortunate to work as a student intern in Professor Diyi Yang’s SALT lab at Stanford University and Professor Yi Wu’s group at IIIS.

<!-- Research Interests: LLM Agent-->
My research interests lie in the intersection of LLM and RL, with a particular focus on Large Language Model (LLM) agents. I am passionate about exploring how LLMs can be effectively integrated with RL techniques to create intelligent agents capable of complex decision-making and problem-solving.
